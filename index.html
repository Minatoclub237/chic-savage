How early should I arrive for my appointment?

Please arrive 10–15 minutes early so you can settle in, enjoy a herbal tea and fill in a short wellness form before your treatment.

What should I wear during a massage?

Undress to your level of comfort. You stay covered with warm linens at all times, and only the area being treated is uncovered.

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Yes. We use gentle, fragrance-free options on request and adapt every facial after a quick skin consultation.

Can I book a treatment for two?

Absolutely — our duo suite lets you and a partner or friend enjoy side-by-side massages and facials.

Do you offer gift cards?

Yes, gift cards are available for any treatment or amount, delivered by email or as a beautiful printed card.

What is your cancellation policy?

You can reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment.
